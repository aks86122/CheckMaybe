# Views aren't income (Threads text, draft for 2026-10-25)

Source: a public Threads post (a creator's first-month Facebook analytics: 2.5M views, $696.32 approximate earnings, 28 days) and the replies under it, seen 2026-10-17. Numbers below are as posted by those users; not verified with Facebook. No one is named. Per-1,000-views figures are our own division.

## Threads
```
Same views. Very different pay.

From one thread about Facebook payouts, numbers as people posted them:
2.5M views, 28 days → $696 (about $0.28 per 1,000 views)
3M views → $250 (about $0.08)
4M views in a month → $173 (about $0.04)

Big view counts aren't income. Before you chase them, ask what one view is worth for your kind of content and audience.
```
