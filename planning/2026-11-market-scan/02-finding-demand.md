# Finding what the US market wants (2026-11-18, J: "many things miss the target")

## What the numbers say so far
- Oct: Gumroad 74 views, 0 sales, 0 free downloads. Too little traffic to judge any product; but 0 free downloads also means the pages/promises didn't convert the few who came.
- Pinterest (10/18): 365 impressions, 7 outbound clicks. Substack (11/2): 1 subscriber.
- So: not "the market rejected us" yet. "Almost nobody has seen us, and we don't know which promise pulls."

## Likely misses (Claude's read, to test, not facts)
1. Two audiences at once (Etsy sellers' licensing + household money). Nobody knows who CheckMaybe is for.
2. The brand sells "check the fine print" (a skeptic's job). People pay for an easy, pleasant result (a tracker they color in, a plan that says "debt-free by Dec 2027"); the fine print works better as free content.
3. Look: top debt/budget printables on Etsy and Pinterest are mostly light, simple, cute. Ours are mostly dark and analytical. Unverified until J's screenshots.
4. Seller-licensing questions are searched, but sellers expect free answers; low paying signal (Reddit notes 2026-10-24: none).

## Method: copy what buyers already pay for, then test before building
1. Etsy (US): search "debt payoff tracker printable", "budget planner 2027", "paycheck budget spreadsheet". Screenshot the first 2 pages: title, price, style, review count, "Bestseller" badge. Open the top 3: read 10 reviews each (what they praise = what they buy) and the 1 to 3 star ones (gaps).
2. Pinterest Trends (US): the same keywords plus "sinking funds", "no spend challenge": rising or flat.
3. Reddit (J pastes, as before): r/debtfree, r/povertyfinance, r/personalfinance threads on BNPL, debt order, holiday debt. Their words become titles.
4. Test the promise with pins before building: 3 pins, same link, different promise or look; 2 weeks; keep the one with the most outbound clicks. We already have dark (bf, tg) and light (pt) pins live: compare their outbound clicks first.
5. Rule: build or rework only what got clicks; stop what got none after 3 to 4 weeks.
