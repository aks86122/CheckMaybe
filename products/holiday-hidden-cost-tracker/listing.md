## Description
```
Holiday shopping rarely ends in December. Pay-in-4, store financing and "0% for 12 months" split one price into small payments, and several small plans tend to land in the same months.

This tracker adds them up, so you can see January's total before you check out.

WHAT IT DOES
• Gift budget: planned vs spent vs left, and how many gifts are sorted.
• Holiday payment plans: enter the price, the payment and how many payments. It works out the total, the extra cost and the real APR hidden in the monthly price.
• Month by month: what all your holiday plans charge in December, January, February and beyond, as a share of your take-home pay.
• Free trials: cancel-by date and countdown for holiday streaming and shipping trials.
• Flags: HIGH (20%+ real APR), CHECK (10%+), OK. Levels are adjustable.
• Dashboard with charts, filters and colour-coded tags.

EASY TO USE
• Google Sheets (free Google account) or Excel.
• Gold headers = you fill in. Berry headers = calculated for you.
• Works on the Google Sheets phone app.

WHAT YOU GET
A 4-page PDF with your link to make your own copy of the tracker, plus a quick how-to.

PLEASE NOTE
• Digital product, nothing is shipped.
• Estimates only, not financial advice. Real APR excludes fees charged separately; your agreement is the final word.
• Personal use only.
```
