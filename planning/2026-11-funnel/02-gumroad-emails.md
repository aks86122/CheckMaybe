## 2. Workflows（Gumroad › Emails › Workflows › New workflow）

### A. 免費小書 Before You Click "Pay Later" → Hidden Cost Tracker
Trigger：Purchase of Before You Click "Pay Later"

**A1　3天後**
- Subject：`Did the 5 checks change anything?`
- Body：
```
Hi,

A few days ago you downloaded Before You Click "Pay Later". Quick reminder of the checks that catch the most people:

1. What will I pay in total? (payment × number of payments)
2. When does the first payment leave?
3. What else is due that month?

Doing this by hand for every plan gets old fast. Hidden Cost Tracker does it in Google Sheets: enter the price, payment and number of payments, and it works out the real APR, what's due each month and which free trials need cancelling.

Hidden Cost Tracker: https://checkmaybe.gumroad.com/l/kofwfjf

Estimates only, not financial advice.
CheckMaybe
```

**A2　7天後**
- Subject：`Snowball, avalanche, or something else?`
- Body：
```
Hi,

If you have more than one debt, the order you pay them in changes how long it takes and how much interest you pay. Rules in your contracts can change the answer too: early-payoff fees, lock-in periods, 0% deals that end.

Debt Payoff Planner compares 4 payoff orders side by side in Google Sheets and checks those rules. It also prints a one-page color-in tracker made from your own plan, with the real amount in every box.

Debt Payoff Planner: https://checkmaybe.gumroad.com/l/ddktis

Free weekly notes on the fine print: checkmaybe.substack.com

Estimates only, not financial advice.
CheckMaybe
```
