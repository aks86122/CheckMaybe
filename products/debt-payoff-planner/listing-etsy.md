## Description (first sentence 126/160 characters, keyword-first)
```
Debt payoff planner for Google Sheets: compare snowball, avalanche and 2 more orders, with early-payoff fees and 0% deadlines.

Snowball or avalanche? The answer can change once real contract rules come in.

In the made-up example debts, Avalanche pays the least interest on paper (1,598.68 vs 1,679.15), but it clears the store card after its 0% deal ends, so back-interest would be added. The Smart order clears it in time.

If you're tired of…
• payoff calculators that assume every debt can be paid early, for free
• not knowing whether an early-payoff fee is worth paying
• missing a 0% promo deadline and getting hit with back-interest
• guessing which debt to put your extra money on first
…this planner is for you.

What Debt Payoff Planner helps you do
✔ Compare 4 payoff orders side by side: Smart, Avalanche (highest APR first), Snowball (smallest balance first) and Cash-flow (frees up monthly money first)
✔ See your debt-free month, total interest, early-payoff fees and first debt cleared for each order
✔ Check if an early-payoff fee is worth it: the fee is compared with the interest you'd save, and extra payments wait if the fee costs more
✔ Keep locked or "not sure" debts on minimums until they unlock
✔ Track 0% and promo deals: the rate switches on the end date, and you get a warning if an order misses a deferred-interest deadline
✔ Put debts that are behind on payments first
✔ Plan a one-time extra payment (bonus, tax refund) in the month you choose
✔ See what you save compared with paying minimums only

What you get
✔ PDF guide with your "Make a copy" link and setup steps
✔ Google Sheets planner, dark theme: Start Here, Plan, My Debts, Schedule
✔ Room for 12 debts and a 30-year month-by-month schedule
✔ 2 charts: total left over time, and interest + fees by order
✔ Checks for a budget below your minimums and minimums that don't cover interest
✔ Made-up example debts so you can see how it works

How it works
1. Open the PDF, click the link and choose "Make a copy". It saves to your own Google Drive. A free Google account is all you need.
2. On My Debts, enter each debt's name, balance, APR and minimum payment. Add fees, lock dates or promo dates only if you have them.
3. On Plan, enter what you can pay toward all debts each month and compare the 4 orders.

Frequently asked questions
1) Do I need Excel or paid software?
No. It's made for Google Sheets, which is free. Prefer Excel? Download your copy as .xlsx (File › Download › Microsoft Excel). The formulas still work.
2) Is my information private?
Your copy lives in your own Google Drive. I can't see what you enter.
3) Does it connect to my bank or make payments?
No. It only uses the numbers you type in.
4) What if I leave the optional columns blank?
Blank means no fee, no lock, no promo and not behind. The maths still works, and blank rows anywhere are fine.
5) My currency isn't US dollars.
It works in any currency. Use the same one for every debt.
6) Will anything be shipped?
No. It's a digital download: a PDF with the link to your copy.

Good to know
• All figures are estimates to help you plan. Not financial, legal or tax advice.
• Interest is worked out monthly at APR ÷ 12. Back-interest, late fees and rate changes you don't enter are not included. Your lender's figures are the final word.
• Example debts and numbers in the images are made up.
• For personal use. Please don't share or resell the template link.
```
