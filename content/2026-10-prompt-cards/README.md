## 10/10 prompts
### Threads
```
Every "passive income prompt" asks AI to play a world-class entrepreneur and build you a money system.

AI is great at sounding confident. It rarely tells you on its own that your product might break a license, or that fees will eat a third of a small sale.

So ask the boring question first:
"I made this with [template / font / AI image]. What license questions do I need to answer before I sell it? Don't assume it's allowed."

A boring answer now beats a takedown notice later.
```
### IG caption
```
Most "AI side hustle prompts" ask AI how to make money.

These 5 ask what could go wrong first: whether you're allowed to sell it, what fees take, your real hourly pay, whether anyone will buy, and when to stop.

Copy them, swap the [brackets] for your own details.

AI is a checklist, not a lawyer or an accountant. Confirm license terms and fees on the original pages before you list anything.

Save this for your next product idea.
```
### FB
```
Before you sell your first digital product, ask AI these 5 questions. Copy them and replace the [brackets].

1. Can I actually sell this?
"I made a product using [what I used]. List every license question I need to answer before selling it commercially, and where in the license to look. Don't assume it's allowed."

2. What will the fees eat?
"I plan to sell on [platform] at [price]. List every fee that could apply and show what I keep per sale. Tell me which numbers to confirm on the platform's fee page."

3. What's my real hourly pay?
"It takes me [hours] to build this, plus [hours per week] for marketing and support. At [price], show conservative, normal and optimistic cases for the first 3 months."

4. Is anyone going to buy it?
"Design a 7-day test, with no ad spend, to check whether [buyer] would pay for [product]. What counts as a pass?"

5. When do I stop?
"I can lose at most [max loss] and 3 months. Write 3 stop rules."

AI can misread a license or a fee page, so always check the original text before you list anything.
```

## 10/12 Pay Later prompts
### Threads
```
"Only $67 a month" is a sentence designed to stop you doing math.

Paste this into any AI before you click:
"I can buy something that costs [price] as [number] payments of [payment]. Work out the total I pay, the extra over the price, and the APR. Show the math."

A $1,200 phone as 24 × $67 comes out around 29.8% APR. The monthly number never tells you that.
```
### IG caption
```
Before you click "Pay Later", ask AI 4 questions: the real APR, what happens if you pay late, what you'll owe every month with all your plans together, and whether you need it now.

Copy the prompts, swap the [brackets], and check the numbers against the plan's own terms.

Estimates only, not financial advice. Free "Before You Click Pay Later" guide: link in bio.
```
