# Can I Sell This? — Gumroad / Beacons listing (v3.1)

## Title
Can I Sell This? — A Commercial Use Toolkit for Canva Sellers

## Summary (short description / "You'll get" line)
A 27-page PDF decision guide for selling products you designed in Canva: check the license rules before you list, with the official clause cited for each answer.

## Description

**You designed it in Canva. Can you sell it?**

It depends on what you used and what the buyer gets. A finished PDF, a printed mug, an editable template and a client logo are four different licensing situations. Mix one Pro element, one Education item or one Branded graphic into a design, and the rules for the whole design can change.

**Can I Sell This?** gives you a first-pass signal for your product (GREEN-LEANING, AMBER or RED), the reason behind it, and the official clause it comes from, so you can fix problems before you list instead of after a takedown.

**What’s inside (27 pages)**
- **The 5-minute pre-publish audit**: five questions to run on every product.
- **Decision path (A–E)**: from “Does the design contain Canva library Content?” to “Could the buyer extract it as a standalone item?”
- **Quick map**: finished PDFs, printed merchandise, editable templates, client designs and standalone asset resale, each with a first-pass signal.
- **Read the label before you design**: what Free, Pro, AI-generated, Education, Branded, Disney, Editorial Use Only and Popular Music labels mean for selling, and when the license starts.
- **10 rule cards**: content labels, Free vs. Pro, finished vs. editable, template rules, merchandise and POD, ebooks and PDFs, client work, logos and trademarks, special labels, and AI-generated content. Each cites the clause it comes from.
- **20 real-world seller scenarios**, each with a signal, the reason, what to check and what could change the answer: printable planners, editable templates, T-shirts and mugs, ebook covers and interiors, client packs and logos, templates with Free or Pro Content, stock photo printables, course workbooks, lead magnets, PLR/MRR bundles, presentation and business card templates, website graphics, printed books and digital sticker packs.
- **Etsy checks and what’s at stake**: five Etsy checks for Canva-made products, and what can happen on the Canva side if a product breaks the license.
- **Pre-publish checklist** (10 items) and **stop-before-you-publish red flags** (7 items).
- **Three worksheets**: a license source tracker, a product audit worksheet and a buyer-facing rights statement builder.
- **A safer product-building hierarchy** and an **official sources page** with check dates.

**What’s new in v3.1 (September 2026)**
- Checked claim by claim against Canva’s Content License Agreement, Licensing Explained page and Terms of Use (effective 19 August 2026), plus Etsy’s Creativity Standards, on 25 September 2026.

**Who it’s for**
Etsy, Gumroad and print-on-demand sellers, template shops, course creators and freelancers who design products or client work in Canva.
