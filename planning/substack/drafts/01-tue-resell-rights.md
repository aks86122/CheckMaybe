## Title
Bought a pack with "resell rights"? 5 lines to check before you list it

## Subtitle
The license you bought is only half the story. The store you sell on has its own rules.

## Body
```
"Full resell rights. Rebrand it. Keep 100% of the profit."

If you've bought a digital pack with a line like that, here's the part the sales page usually skips. The place you plan to sell it has its own rules, and those rules come first.

I went through the rules of three stores and the Canva license, and pulled out five lines. Each one is quoted word for word, with the date shown on the page.

Start with Gumroad. Its Prohibited Products page, last revised 16 Sep 2026, includes this line:

> "reselling private label rights products"

It names PLR. It doesn't name "master resell rights" or plain "resell rights", which doesn't make those safe. It just means you can't read the answer off this one line, and Gumroad's own page says that if you're unsure, you should contact them.

Beacons is more direct. Its Community Standards, updated 22 Nov 2025, say:

> "In general, we do not allow resale; you cannot sell products you did not create."

The same page lets you act as an affiliate for other creators. Selling the product as your own is the part it rules out.

Etsy comes at it from another angle. Its Creativity Standards, updated 10 Jun 2025, say items "designed by a seller" "include only" a few listed types, and give this as an example of something that doesn't qualify:

> "A bundle, collection, scan, or PDF of someone else's work"

A resell pack is, by definition, someone else's work. A new cover doesn't change who made it.

Then there's what's inside the pack. Many resell packs are Canva templates, and section 9 of Canva's Content License Agreement lists things you can't do with Free or Pro content, including:

> "sub-license, re-sell, rent, lend, assign, gift or otherwise transfer or distribute the Content or the rights granted under this Content License Agreement"

There's a narrow exception in section 4A, but the point stands: a pack seller can't hand you rights to the Canva photos, graphics or fonts inside it, however the sales page words it.

A few lines further down, the same section says you can't:

> "use or display Content in a manner that gives the impression that the Content was created by you"

That's the exact promise many packs make. Put your name on it.

So before you list, find the rules page of the store you'll sell on and search it for "resell", "PLR" and "private label". Open the files and look at which parts are Canva library content, and whether a buyer could pull them out. Keep the parts you made yourself, your own text, graphics and layouts, because that's the safer ground. And if the answer is still unclear, ask the platform before you list, not after a takedown.

Rules change, so check the current pages yourself. This is educational, not legal advice, and I'm not affiliated with Gumroad, Beacons, Etsy or Canva.

```
