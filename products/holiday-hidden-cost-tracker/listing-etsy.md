## Description (first sentence 132/160 characters, keyword-first)
```
Christmas budget planner for Google Sheets: gift list, holiday BNPL payment planner and free-trial tracker, with your January bills.

Holiday shopping doesn't end in December.

In the made-up example, four holiday plans (two pay-in-4, store financing and a monthly plan) still add $428.25 to January. The game console bought as 6 × $89.50 on a $499 price works out to about 25.7% APR.

If you're tired of…
• small pay-in-4 payments piling up in the same month
• not knowing what January will cost until the bills arrive
• going over your gift budget without noticing
• holiday free trials turning into charges
…this tracker is for you.

What Holiday Hidden Cost Tracker helps you do
✔ Keep your gift budget in check: planned, spent, left and gifts sorted
✔ See what all your holiday plans charge each month, for 5 months, as a share of your take-home pay
✔ Work out the real APR and extra cost of any plan: enter the price, the payment and the number of payments
✔ Spot the expensive ones: HIGH / CHECK / OK flag on every plan (20% and 10% by default, change them in Settings)
✔ Track every gift: who it's for, budget vs actual cost, how you paid and status
✔ Never miss a cancel-by date: countdown for every free trial and the yearly cost if you keep it

What you get
✔ PDF guide with your "Make a copy" link and setup steps
✔ Google Sheets tracker, dark green and gold: Dashboard, Gift List, Holiday Payments, Free Trials, Settings, How to Use
✔ Dashboard with budget cards, a month-by-month table and 2 charts
✔ Tick boxes, filter bars and colour tags
✔ Made-up example rows so you can see how it works

How it works
1. Open the PDF, click the link and choose "Make a copy". It saves to your own Google Drive. A free Google account is all you need.
2. In Settings, enter your holiday budget, monthly take-home pay and the first month to plan.
3. Add your gifts, payment plans and free trials. The Dashboard fills in on its own.

Frequently asked questions
1) Do I need Excel or paid software?
No. It's made for Google Sheets, which is free. Prefer Excel? Download your copy as .xlsx (File › Download › Microsoft Excel). The formulas still work; tick boxes turn into TRUE/FALSE cells.
2) Is my information private?
Your copy lives in your own Google Drive. I can't see what you enter.
3) Does it connect to my bank or cancel trials for me?
No. It only uses the numbers you type in.
4) I already have Hidden Cost Tracker. Do I need this?
This is its holiday edition: the same real-APR maths, built around gifts and the January bills.
5) My currency isn't US dollars.
Select the money columns and change the currency under Format › Number. The maths stays the same.
6) Will anything be shipped?
No. It's a digital download: a PDF with the link to your copy.

Good to know
• All figures are estimates to help you plan. Not financial, legal or tax advice.
• Real APR is estimated from your payment schedule and doesn't include fees charged separately. 0% offers can add back-dated interest if a payment is late: check your agreement.
• Example numbers in the images are made up.
• For personal use. Please don't share or resell the template link.
```
