```
"For personal use only" isn't one rule.

In Canva's license, Branded Content is "personal use only" and Education Content is "non-commercial use only". When a design mixes them, the most restrictive license "applies to the entire design".

So one small element can make a whole template unsellable. Open the info panel of every element you didn't make before you list.

Not legal advice. Check the current terms yourself.
```
