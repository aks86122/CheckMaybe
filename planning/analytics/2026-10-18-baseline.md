## Reading
- Volume is too small to judge the products: under 100 product views in a month. At a typical 1–3% rate, one sale needs roughly 100–300 views.
- The free guide got 0 downloads, so the first problem is before the price: people don't reach or don't take the free offer.
- Instagram and Threads visitors do click (43% and 71%), but there are few of them. Facebook visitors on Beacons never click (0 of 23).
- Pinterest: the pins ARE live (profile screenshot 2026-10-18, about 30 pins), but they only send ~1 visit to Gumroad and ~1 to Beacons. Each pin has 9–51 impressions so far; normal for an account a few weeks old.
- Traffic stopped when posting stopped (after Oct 12). The 10/19–11/1 daily test will show whether posting volume moves it.

## Changes (founder to do; suggested 2026-10-18)
1. Beacons: put the free Pay Later guide as the first link, above "See all toolkits".
2. Facebook posts: link straight to the free guide on Gumroad (with UTM), not the Beacons page.
3. Every link in a post or bio gets a UTM (`utm_source=threads|instagram|facebook|pinterest&utm_campaign=<post date>`), so Gumroad shows which post worked.
4. Pinterest: v1 pins are already up. Add the 4 v2 pins at 1 a day; make 2–3 more versions of the "Only $67 a month" pin (best so far). Open one pin and check its link is the Gumroad URL with the UTM.
5. Weekly check, every Sunday: Beacons views and clicks, Gumroad views, free-guide downloads. Compare with this file on 11/1.
