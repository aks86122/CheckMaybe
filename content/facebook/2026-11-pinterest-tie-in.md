```
Most debt payoff printables give every box the same $100.

Real debts don't work like that. A card at 24% and a store card at 0% that ends in May shouldn't share one tracker with equal boxes.

So I made the printable come from the plan itself: your debts in payoff order, the real amount in each box, and the month you'll reach every 5% of your total. Change a number and the page updates. Print it, put it on the fridge, color a box when you pay.

One thing to check before you pick snowball or avalanche: when does any 0% deal end, and does it have deferred interest? That can flip which order is cheapest.

More on that in the first comment 👇
```
