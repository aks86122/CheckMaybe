## Findings
- Buyers searching these words on Etsy want assets (PLR bundles, templates), not rules. No licensing-rules guide like CheckMaybe appears on any page 1.
- Guides that sell on Etsy teach making money (US$3–15, Bestseller).
- "ai commercial license" is a different market: designers selling licences for their own work (STL files, wall art), NT$140–20,000.
- A "Canva Pro Lifetime Access / Team Invite" listing appears on page 1 of "digital products", "canva commercial use" and "canva pro lifetime" → new scenarios in Can I Sell This? v3.2.
