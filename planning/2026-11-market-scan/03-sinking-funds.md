## What the snapshots show
- Many listings: printable PDF, Canva, Word, Google Sheets; A4, Letter, A5, A6.
- Prices: about $0.75 to $8.50, most $1 to $3, heavy "sale" pricing.
- Top seller in the results: 4.8 stars (4.2k reviews) · 51k sales. This is most likely the shop's total, not this one listing. Most other listings: 3 to 5 reviews.
- Listings mention "Dave Ramsey" (envelope / baby-steps audience).

## Read
- Demand: real (the keyword has its own Etsy market page, and one shop has 51k sales).
- Price: very low; a static printable is a $1 to $3 commodity. A standalone CheckMaybe printable would compete on price with thousands of listings.
- Possible edge (what CheckMaybe already does): a Google Sheet that calculates. For each goal: amount and due date → how much per month or per paycheck, whether the total fits the budget, and which yearly bills people forget (car registration, insurance, annual subscriptions). Static printables don't do this.
- Overlap: Hidden Cost Tracker already turns subscriptions into yearly totals, so a sinking-funds tab could be added there instead of a new product.
