# "0% interest*": read the asterisk (Threads, 2026-10-23)
Added 2026-10-17 to fill 10/23 after comic #01 moved to 10/21. Fact source: US CFPB on deferred interest (same as Pinterest pin m3). No numbers.
```
"0% interest for 12 months*"

Before you agree, look for two phrases in the fine print:
"deferred interest" or "no interest if paid in full."

If any balance is left when the promo ends, interest can be charged going back to the day you bought it.

A true 0% plan doesn't work that way. Read the * first.

Educational, not financial advice.
```
