# Human-made sellers: product plan v0.1 (2026-11-01)

Founder 2026-11-01: write this up as a plan and produce the test posts first.

## Why
Three r/EtsyCommunity threads (read 2026-11-01) show pains CheckMaybe's "rules in plain words" can answer, from a group the current products don't serve: handmade and pattern sellers, many of them anti-AI.

| Pain | Where (public comments, paraphrased) |
| --- | --- |
| Own work flagged as AI | Canva background remover → Instagram AI label (WendyNPeterPan); Etsy's new image detection "flags photos for being modified by AI when they are not" (SkeptiBee) |
| No way to prove it if reported | "If I use something not generative and someone reports me how do I defend myself?" (alcaron) |
| Designs copied with AI | sewing pattern seller: copies of their patterns made with AI, "left completely unprotected by Etsy" (No-Masterpiece-5708) |
| Buyers can't tell it's handmade | cross stitch seller, test stitches everything, no AI, 0 sales in 3 months |

Evidence is a handful of comments. Test before building.

## Audience note
r/EtsyCommunity bans AI-generated posts/comments (rule 2) and any promotion of own tools (rule 5). It is not a channel for Can I Use This? or for these products. Reddit stays: real answers in your own words, no links, no CheckMaybe.

## Products
### A. "Prove It's Human-Made" kit (recommended first)
For handmade sellers and pattern makers.
- Etsy's October 2026 photo rules: what can be edited, which photo must stay unedited (quotes from Listing Image Requirements and "Important updates to AI use on Etsy").
- Evidence habit: original phone file, process photos, source files (chart, sketch, draft) with dates; what each one proves.
- Why a human-made photo can read as AI: C2PA / Content Credentials markers written by some tools; platforms that read them; what our own Photo Check tests found.
- If a listing is reported as AI: what to prepare and how to respond. **Only if Etsy publishes the process; not found yet.**
- Disclosure and "made by me" wording that stays inside Etsy's rules, with the rule text.
- Free Photo Check as the first step.
Format: start as a free or low-price checklist (Gumroad), grow into a toolkit if the test works.

### B. "My design was copied" Etsy IP report guide (later)
Etsy Intellectual Property Policy: how to report, what evidence, counter-notice. Legal-adjacent, about twice A's verification work. Start only after A has results.

## Changes to existing products
- **Photo Check**: add a plain verdict for Etsy sellers: "Good candidate for Etsy's unedited photo" (camera data, no editing app, no AI marker) vs "Not a good candidate" (edited, AI marker, screenshot, no data). Small change, links straight into A. Any public deploy change needs the founder's OK.
- **Can I Use This?**: no change. Keep it on Pinterest and Substack, where AI-using sellers are.

## Test (before building A)
1. Substack post "Made it by hand and still flagged as AI?" + 1 Note + pin et7 (`content/2026-11-human-made-posts.md`).
2. Measure on 11/8 and again 11/15: post views and new subscribers, pin impressions/saves/outbound clicks, Photo Check visits from `utm_campaign=human-made`. Compare with the Etsy AI training post and pin et6.
3. Go: the post or pin clearly beats et6, or readers reply with their own "flagged as AI" stories → build A as a free checklist first. No-go: drop A, keep the post as content.
