# Can I Resell This? — Gumroad / Beacons listing (v1.0)

## Title
Can I Resell This? — A Buyer’s Checklist Before You Buy Resell Rights

## Summary (short description / "You'll get" line)
A 22-page PDF checklist for anyone about to buy a pack with “resell rights”: what to check before you pay, and again before you list anything from it.

## Description

**The pack says “resell rights”. Does that mean you can sell it?**

Not always. The licence you actually receive, the rules of the platform you sell on, and the content inside the pack are three separate checks. A pack can pass one and fail the others: some platforms name these products in their prohibited lists, and a Canva graphic, stock photo or brand logo inside the pack comes with its own terms.

**Can I Resell This?** walks you through those checks before you buy, and again before you list, with the official wording quoted for each platform rule.

**What’s inside (22 pages)**
- **Key terms**: how sellers commonly use PLR, MRR, Resell Rights and Personal Use, and why the licence text decides, not the label.
- **Before you buy: seven questions**, from “Is there a written licence?” to “Can you get your money back?”
- **Platform rules, word for word**: Gumroad, Beacons and Etsy, quoted from the official pages with their dates.
- **What’s inside the pack**: Canva library content, AI output, stock photos, music and fonts, and brand logos in “luxury” clips.
- **Affiliate vs resell**: why promoting a product with a link is a different route from reselling it.
- **Income screenshots, testimonials and disclosure**: what the US FTC’s own guidance pages say, quoted and dated.
- **15 real-world scenarios**, each with a GREEN-LEANING / AMBER / RED signal, the reason, what to check and what could change the answer.
- **14 red flags before you buy**, a **14-point before-you-list checklist**, two **worksheets** (licence record, platform & rights check) and a **safer-paths** overview.
- **Sources page** with dates for every official page quoted.

**Who it’s for**
Creators thinking about buying a PLR, MRR or resell-rights pack or course, and sellers who already bought one and want to check before they list.

**Good to know**
- Educational resource, not legal advice. The signals are a first pass, not permission.
- **This guide does not grant any resale, PLR or MRR rights.** Personal use only.
- No income claims. It doesn’t judge any seller or course; it shows you what to check.
- Platform rules change. Always confirm the current official terms before you buy or list.
- Independent resource, not affiliated with Gumroad, Beacons, Etsy, Canva or any other company named.

Format: PDF, 22 pages, A4. Instant download.
