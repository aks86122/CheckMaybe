## Post 1 — Platform rules (Threads, 4 parts)

**1/4** (153/500)
```
Bought a pack with “resell rights”?

Before you list it, check the rules of the place you plan to sell it. Here are three platforms, in their own words 👇
```

**2/4** (249/500)
```
Gumroad’s Prohibited Products list (last revised 16 Sep 2026) includes:
“reselling private label rights products”

Beacons’ Community Standards (updated 22 Nov 2025):
“In general, we do not allow resale; you cannot sell products you did not create.”
```

**3/4** (150/500)
```
Etsy’s Creativity Standards (updated 10 Jun 2025) list this as NOT “designed by a seller”:
“A bundle, collection, scan, or PDF of someone else’s work”
```

**4/4** (261/500)

Read the rules of the place you’ll sell, not only the licence. Rules change, so check the current pages yourself. Educational, not legal advice.

## Post 2 — Canva inside resell packs (Threads, 5 parts)

**1/5** (129/500)
```
A lot of “done-for-you” resell packs are Canva templates.

Here’s the line in Canva’s licence that the sales page usually skips 👇
```

**2/5** (289/500)
```
Canva’s Content License Agreement, §9 Prohibited uses. You can’t:
“sub-license, re-sell, rent, lend, assign, gift or otherwise transfer or distribute the Content or the rights granted under this Content License Agreement”
(with a narrow exception in §4A for handing a design to one client)
```

**3/5** (282/500)
```
Same section, a few lines down. You can’t:
“use or display Content in a manner that gives the impression that the Content was created by you”

So a pack that promises “full resell rights, rebrand it as your own” can’t pass on rights to the Canva photos, graphics or fonts inside it.
```

**4/5** (254/500)
```
Before you list anything built on a pack like that, check:
☐ which elements are Canva library content
☐ what label each carries (Free, Pro, Education, Branded)
☐ whether a buyer could pull them out as files
Your own text and graphics are the safer parts.
```
