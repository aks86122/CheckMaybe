## Description
```
Most debt trackers give you snowball and avalanche, and assume every debt can be paid off any time, for free.

Real contracts aren't like that. Some loans charge a fee if you pay early. Some are locked for a while. Some store cards are 0% until a date, then add back-interest from day one if you haven't cleared them.

This planner takes those rules into account.

WHAT IT DOES
• Compares 4 payoff orders: Smart, Avalanche, Snowball and Cash-flow. Debt-free date, total interest, early-payoff fees and first debt cleared, side by side.
• Early-payoff fee check: compares the fee with the interest you'd save. If the fee costs more, extra payments wait until the fee ends.
• Locked or "not sure" debts get minimums only until they unlock.
• 0% / promo deals: the rate switches on the end date, and you get a warning if an order misses a deferred-interest deadline.
• Behind on payments? Smart deals with those first.
• One-time extra payment (bonus, tax refund) in the month you choose.
• Month-by-month schedule for up to 12 debts and 30 years.
• Checks: budget below your minimums, minimum payments that don't cover interest.

EASY TO FILL IN
• Only 4 things are needed per debt: name, balance, APR, minimum payment. Leave the rest blank if it doesn't apply.
• Works in any currency.
• Google Sheets (free Google account) or Excel.

WHAT YOU GET
A 4-page PDF with your link to make your own copy of the planner, plus a quick how-to.

PLEASE NOTE
• Digital product, nothing is shipped.
• Estimates only, not financial advice. Your lender's figures are the final word.
• Personal use only.
```
