# Gumroad checkout upsells (2026-10-26)

Idea from the Starbucks "7 ways to raise revenue" video (upsize / complementary / second cup): offer the matching bundle when someone pays for a single product. Prices read from the live Gumroad store 2026-10-26.

| # | Name | Type of offer | Apply to | Product to offer | Discount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Toolkit → Complete bundle | Replace the selected products with another product | Can I Sell This? ($15), Can I Use This? ($15), Can I Resell This? ($15) | The Complete Toolkit Bundle ($29) | Off |
| 2 | Tracker → Debt Toolkit bundle | Replace the selected products with another product | Hidden Cost Tracker ($8), Debt Payoff Planner ($7), Holiday Hidden Cost Tracker ($7) | Debt Toolkit Bundle ($15) | Off |

Not on: the free products (Listing Photo Checklist, Before You Click "Pay Later", 5-Minute Pre-Publish Audit) and the bundles themselves.

Upsell 1 text
- Offer text: `Get all three toolkits for $29`
- Offer description: `Add Can I Sell This?, Can I Use This? and Can I Resell This? together for $29 instead of $15 each. Same files, one download. Educational resources, not legal advice.`

Upsell 2 text
- Offer text: `Get all three trackers for $15`
- Offer description: `Swap this tracker for the Debt Toolkit Bundle: Hidden Cost Tracker, Debt Payoff Planner and Holiday Hidden Cost Tracker for $15. Google Sheets, also works in Excel. Estimates only, not financial advice.`

Check after a few weeks: Gumroad sales for the two bundles, and whether single-product sales drop.

## Beacons order bumps (2026-10-26)
Beacons has no "replace with bundle" upsell; its Order Bump adds a second digital product at a price you set, on the checkout page. All 10 products are on Beacons (founder screenshots 2026-10-26), including both bundles and the two free PDFs. Bump prices keep "2 items" below the matching 3-item bundle.
