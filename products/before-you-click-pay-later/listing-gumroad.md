## Description (paste, then bold the headings by hand)
```
"Only $67 a month" isn't the whole price.

That $1,200 phone paid as 24 × $67 costs $408 extra, about 29.8% APR (made-up example). The checkout screen shows the monthly payment. This guide shows you the rest, in five minutes.

If you're tired of…
• "small" monthly payments that add up to a big month
• not knowing whether a 0% offer is really 0%
• finding out about a fee or a charge after it's taken
…this guide is for you.

What this guide helps you do
✔ Work out what a plan costs you in total, with one line of maths
✔ See roughly what yearly rate (APR) that is, with four worked examples
✔ Spot "deferred interest" and "no interest if paid in full" before you agree
✔ Know when the first payment leaves, and from which account
✔ Line up every plan so you can see what's due each month
✔ Catch the free trial you started at checkout before it bills

What you get
✔ 12-page PDF, US Letter, dark design
✔ The 5 checks, one per page, with made-up examples
✔ A one-page summary you can screenshot
✔ A fill-in list for all your pay-later plans (works in most PDF apps)
✔ Sources for the rules quoted (US Consumer Financial Protection Bureau)

How it works
1. Get the PDF (free).
2. Next time you see "pay later" at checkout, go through the five checks.
3. Add the plan to your list so nothing lands as a surprise.

Frequently asked questions
1) Is it really free?
Yes. You can pay what you want, but $0 is fine.
2) Does it tell me whether to use a plan?
No. It shows you the full cost so you can decide. It isn't financial advice.
3) Do I need a spreadsheet?
No. A phone calculator is enough. If you'd like it worked out automatically, page 11 links to Hidden Cost Tracker (paid, optional).
4) Can I fill in the list on my phone?
Yes, in most PDF apps (Adobe Acrobat Reader, Apple Books / Files, Google Drive). Save after filling in.
5) Is it about a specific company?
No. It doesn't review or name any pay-later provider. Every plan is different: your agreement is the final word.

Good to know
• All examples are made up. Rates are rounded estimates from the payment schedule and don't include fees charged separately.
• Not financial, legal or tax advice.
• Free to keep and share as a whole; please don't sell it.
```
