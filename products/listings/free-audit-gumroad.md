# The 5-Minute Pre-Publish Audit — Gumroad / Beacons listing (v1.0, free)

## Title
The 5-Minute Pre-Publish Audit — A Free Checklist for Digital Product Sellers

## Summary
A free 6-page checklist: five questions to run before you list any template, printable, ebook or AI-assisted product.

## Description

**Before you hit publish, can you answer these five?**

1. What went into it?
2. Which licence covers each piece?
3. What exactly am I selling?
4. Where will I sell it?
5. What must I disclose?

Most listing problems start with one piece nobody checked: a library graphic, a font, an AI image, or a pack bought with “resell rights”. This free checklist helps you find that piece before you list.

**What’s inside (6 pages)**
- **The five questions**, with a simple GREEN-LEANING / AMBER / RED signal for your answers
- **Red flags**: nine signs to stop and check, with Etsy’s own wording quoted
- **A one-page product record** to note each piece, its licence and the date you checked
- **Where to go deeper**: which CheckMaybe toolkit fits your product

**Who it’s for**
Etsy, Gumroad and print-on-demand sellers, template shops and creators who use Canva or AI tools.

**Good to know**
- Free. Pay what you want if you’d like to support more checklists like this.
- Educational resource, not legal advice. It shows you what to check, not what is permitted.
- Independent resource, not affiliated with any platform named.

Format: PDF, 6 pages, A4. Instant download.
