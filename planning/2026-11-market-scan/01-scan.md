Sources are mostly vendor/SEO blogs: directional only. No Etsy or Gumroad best-seller data was found.

## What the search shows
- Etsy digital: planners, Canva templates, wall art, stickers still the big categories; bundles and niches beat generic items. "digital download" has about 2.2M competing listings, flat interest, half of page 1 is ads (listifyai, insightagent, unilink).

- Adobe 2026 holiday forecast (2026-09-28): US online $275.1B (+6.7%); BNPL $21.3B Nov to Dec (+6.6%), peak $1.09B on Cyber Monday; 82% of BNPL on mobile.
- Pinterest Predicts 2027 not out yet (usually December).
