## Title
Bought a pack with "resell rights"? 5 lines to check before you list it

## Subtitle
The license you bought is only half the story. The store you sell on has its own rules.

## Body
```
"Full resell rights. Rebrand it. Keep 100% of the profit."

If you've bought a digital pack with a line like that, here's the part the sales page usually skips: the place you plan to sell it has its own rules, and those rules come first.

Below are five lines, quoted word for word from the platforms' own pages. Read them before you list anything you didn't make from scratch.

**1. Gumroad: one line in the Prohibited Products list**

Gumroad's Prohibited Products page (last revised 16 Sep 2026) includes:

> "reselling private label rights products"

Note what it does and doesn't say. It names PLR. It doesn't name "master resell rights" or plain "resell rights". That doesn't make those safe; it means you can't read the answer off this line. If you're unsure, Gumroad's own page says: "If you are unsure whether your content is prohibited on Gumroad, please contact us."

**2. Beacons: resale in general**

Beacons' Community Standards (updated 22 Nov 2025):

> "In general, we do not allow resale; you cannot sell products you did not create."

The same page allows you to act as an affiliate for other creators. Selling the product as your own is the part it rules out.

**3. Etsy: what counts as "designed by a seller"**

Etsy's Creativity Standards (updated 10 Jun 2025) say items "designed by a seller" "include only" a few listed types. Among the examples that do not qualify:

> "A bundle, collection, scan, or PDF of someone else's work"

A resell pack is, by definition, someone else's work. A new cover doesn't change who made it.

**4. Canva: the rights inside the pack can't be passed on**

Many resell packs are Canva templates. Canva's Content License Agreement, §9, lists things you can't do with Free or Pro content, including:

> "sub-license, re-sell, rent, lend, assign, gift or otherwise transfer or distribute the Content or the rights granted under this Content License Agreement"

(There's a narrow exception in §4A.) So a pack seller can't hand you rights to the Canva photos, graphics or fonts inside it, however the sales page words it.

**5. Canva again: "rebrand it as your own"**

Same section, a few lines down. You can't:

> "use or display Content in a manner that gives the impression that the Content was created by you"

That's the exact promise many packs make: put your name on it.

**What to do before you list**

- Find the rules page of the store you'll sell on and search it for "resell", "PLR" and "private label".
- Open the files: which parts are Canva library content, and could a buyer pull them out?
- Keep the parts you made yourself. Your own text, your own graphics and your own layouts are the safer ground.
- If the answer is unclear, ask the platform before you list, not after a takedown.

Rules change, so check the current pages yourself. Educational, not legal advice. Not affiliated with Gumroad, Beacons, Etsy or Canva.
```
